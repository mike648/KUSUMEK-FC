OUR CLUB.
OUR PEOPLE.

Built on passion. Driven by the community.

KUSUMEK FC is a local football club based in Kusumek, Chemosot Ward, Kericho County. We bring people together through the beautiful game, creating opportunities for players to grow, compete and represent our community with pride.
